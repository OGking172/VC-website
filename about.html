About us

Our legacy of quality.

At VC Custom Homes, we've never believed in shortcuts. For more than 25 years, we've been building custom and spec homes throughout Upstate South Carolina with one guiding principle: treat every home like it's our own.

Crafting homes that fit your budget

We understand that building your dream home doesn't have to come with a sky-high price tag. We take pride in offering a range of options that allow you to create a home that matches your needs, vision, and budget. From carefully sourced materials to thoughtful designs, we'll help you get more for your money without compromising on the things that matter most.
